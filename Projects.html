MiningIncremental

Page Github

WORK IN PROGRESS

This is an incremental clicker game where you collect different minerals like wood, stone, iron, and gold by clicking buttons and then sell them for money. You can spend money on upgrades that automatically generate more minerals over time, and progressively unlock stronger minerals and upgrade tiers as you advance. The goal is to build up your resource production and economy through smart upgrading and unlocking.

Slasher

Page Github

WORK IN PROGRESS

This is a stage-based action game where you fight enemies with limited health while trying to survive with only three hit points of your own. Each new stage increases the size of the map, making battles more challenging as you progress. You move using WASD and can perform powerful slashing attacks by holding space and moving in a direction, letting you deal damage while dashing across the map.
